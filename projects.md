# Hobbies & Projects

Welcome to the workshop! Here are a few of the things keeping me busy:

*   **[Web Applications](/apps/):** Custom client-side tools built using the Google Antigravity editor framework. *(Click to view the apps!)*
*   **Barebow Archery:** Tuning equipment and running drills to dial in form and alignment for indoor competitive distances.
*   **3D Resin Printing:** Designing highly precise, custom parametric tools using OpenSCAD scripting.
*   **Indoor Greenery:** Managing passive hydroponic setups and specialized grow lights for an extensive collection, including my unique multi-lobed plants.
*   **Billiards:** Running progressive table control drills and focusing on the physics of stroke precision.
